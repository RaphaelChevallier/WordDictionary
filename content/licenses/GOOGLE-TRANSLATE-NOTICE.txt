Optional definition translation uses the installed Android on-device translation
service when a ready language pair is available. The optional fallback uses
Google Translate through the ML Kit on-device Translation API.

Definition text is translated on the device. Language model downloads contact
Google. Automatic translations may be inaccurate; the original dictionary
definition remains available.

ML Kit sends SDK performance, usage, device and per-installation diagnostics,
including configured source and target languages, to Google. It may also contact
Google for SDK configuration and compatibility information. Google states that
input text and translated results are processed on-device and are not sent to
Google servers.
